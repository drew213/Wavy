## Steps

1. Export the verified report (HTML and PDF). Share it privately. Never post a client report publicly.
2. Run the readout:
   - **Snapshot** (30 min): the scorecard, the top 3 fixes and what they're worth, what we couldn't see, and the Deep audit / retainer offer.
   - **Deep** (60 min): the scorecard, the top-10 roadmap, the first 30 days of work, open client inputs, and the re-audit date.
3. Record decisions and owners in the tracker notes and in ClickUp.
4. Send a short written recap the same day: what we found, what we agreed, what we need from them, and dates.

## Guardrails

- Every message to the client is reviewed by the account lead before it goes out.
- No live changes start until the client approves the specific change in writing.
